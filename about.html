About DigitalSaathi

DigitalSaathi is your independent, 100% free online tools platform for everyday digital tasks. Our mission is to provide fast, privacy-first, and easy-to-use tools that let you convert, compress, edit, calculate, and manage your digital files directly in your web browser.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, privacy-first, and completely free online tools that empower everyone to work with PDFs, images, code, text, and calculations without server upload risks or subscriptions.

Privacy by Design

Unlike traditional online tools that upload your documents to remote servers, DigitalSaathi processes all your files directly inside your browser sandbox using modern WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and Web APIs. Your confidential files never leave your computer.

What We Offer

We provide a comprehensive suite of tools covering PDF utilities, photo and image compression, developer data formatters, financial and educational calculators, text analysis, and security utilities. Everything is fast, free, and unlimited!
