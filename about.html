About Vytra

Dedicated to Fast, Private, In-Browser Productivity

Vytra is an independent online tools platform created to solve everyday digital file and data formatting challenges for students, job applicants, software engineers, and cyber café businesses worldwide.

Our Core Mission

Most commercial online file converters require users to upload confidential documents, personal marksheets, or identity cards to third-party cloud servers. These services often impose restrictive daily limits, watermark your files, or gate basic features behind expensive monthly subscriptions.

Our mission is straightforward: empower users with 100% free, private, high-performance tools that execute entirely in the web browser.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Your PDF documents, photos, tokens, and calculations never leave your computer. Everything runs in local browser memory.

Zero Cost & Unlimited

No subscriptions, no hidden daily task caps, and no forced user sign-ups or email collection.

Instant Browser Speed

Powered by native WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Workers for zero-network execution speed.

How We Maintain This Platform

Vytra is hosted on GitHub Pages and maintained as an independent project. To fund development, keep all 67+ tools completely free, and ensure ongoing updates without paywalls, we display clean, non-intrusive advertisements served through trusted partners like Google AdSense.

We strictly prioritize user experience: ads are positioned away from interactive tool dropzones and buttons to prevent accidental clicks or disruption.

What Tools We Offer

Our platform includes 6 primary operational categories:

  • PDF Suite (37 tools): Merge, split, compress to 100KB, convert to Word/Excel/JPG, sign, protect, and edit.
  • Image Suite (19 tools): Compress under 20KB/50KB, resize to exact exam pixels, crop passport photos, and convert formats.
  • Calculators (5 tools): Loan EMI with amortization schedules, marks percentage, exact chronological age, and college attendance.
  • Developer Tools (3 tools): Format JSON, beautify complex SQL queries, and encode Base64 strings.
  • Text Tools (1 tool): Real-time word, character, and reading time analyzer.
  • Utilities (2 tools): Permanent UPI and WiFi QR code generator, and cryptographically secure password generator.
