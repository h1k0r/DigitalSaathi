About DigitalSaathi

100% Free, Private & Client-Side Digital Utility Suite

DigitalSaathi (hosted at vytra.in) is an independent web toolkit engineered specifically to assist Indian students, competitive examination candidates, cyber café operators, and everyday internet users in formatting, converting, and securing their digital files.

Core Privacy Principle: Every tool on DigitalSaathi executes 100% inside your web browser. Your confidential documents, marksheets, certificates, photos, and signatures never leave your device and are never uploaded to any remote server.

Why We Built DigitalSaathi

Across India, millions of students and job applicants apply annually for government examinations (such as SSC, UPSC, State PSCs, IBPS, Railways, CTET, and NEET) as well as university admissions. These application portals mandate strict, precise file formatting rules:

  • Passport photos strictly between 20 KB and 50 KB with exact pixel dimensions.
  • Signatures compressed under 20 KB on pure white backgrounds.
  • Mark sheets, certificates, and ID proofs merged into single PDFs under 100 KB or 200 KB.

Most commercial online file converters force users to upload sensitive personal documents to unknown cloud servers, impose arbitrary daily quotas, or place basic editing features behind paid monthly subscriptions.

DigitalSaathi was created to remove these barriers: providing a completely free, fast, and privacy-respecting alternative that works smoothly even on budget smartphones and cyber café desktop browsers.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Processed locally via WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Workers. Zero file uploads, zero server storage, zero data leakage.

Zero Cost & Unlimited

No subscriptions, no hidden daily task limits, no watermarks, and no mandatory account registration.

Tailored for India Needs

Built-in presets for Indian government exams, cyber café print layouts, state certificates, and UPI QR codes.

How the Technology Works

Instead of transferring large files over the network to a central backend cluster, DigitalSaathi runs modern JavaScript libraries and WebAssembly binaries directly within your web browser engine:

  • PDF Operations: Compiled client-side PDF rendering and manipulation engines handle merging, splitting, page extraction, and compression on your local CPU.
  • Image Manipulation: High-performance HTML5 Canvas APIs perform exact pixel cropping, DPI adjustments, and multi-pass quantization to reach target KB sizes without quality degradation.
  • Calculators & Utilities: Deterministic mathematical models compute financial EMIs, age differentials, and academic CGPA conversions instantaneously with zero latency.

Our Sustainability & Ad Policy

DigitalSaathi is an independently maintained web project. To fund development, cover hosting infrastructure, and keep every single tool accessible to all users for free, we display clean, non-intrusive advertisements provided by Google AdSense.

We strictly design ad placements to avoid interfering with utility workflows: ads are positioned cleanly away from file dropzones, action buttons, and conversion controls to ensure a seamless experience.
