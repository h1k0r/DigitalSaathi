Frequently Asked Questions

Does reordering PDF pages reduce visual sharpness or compress text?
No. Vytra rearranges the internal page tree references inside the PDF structure using pdf-lib without re-rendering or compressing text, images, or vector shapes.
Can I reverse the entire page order in one click?
Yes! Click the '🔄 Reverse All Pages' button on the toolbar to immediately flip the document sequence from back to front.
Is there a limit on how many pages I can reorder?
No. Vytra operates directly in your browser memory and can process documents with dozens or hundreds of pages without subscription limits.
Are my confidential files uploaded or saved on any server?
Never. All page rendering and restructuring happen 100% locally in your device RAM. Your files never touch an external server.
