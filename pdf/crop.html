Frequently Asked Questions

Does cropping PDF margins degrade image or text sharpness?
No. Vytra updates the standard PDF CropBox boundary coordinates via pdf-lib. All vector fonts, selectable text layers, and embedded high-resolution graphics remain 100% untouched without lossy bitmap recompression.
Can I crop the current page only or all pages in the PDF?
You can do both! In the settings pane, select 'All Pages in Document' to apply uniform border trims across the entire file, or choose 'Current Page Only' to crop a specific cover page or appendix.
Why is cropping PDF margins helpful for Kindle and e-readers?
Academic papers and book scans often feature huge 2-inch white margins. Trimming these margins allows e-reader software to zoom in and expand the body text to fill the screen, dramatically improving reading comfort.
Are my private documents uploaded to an external server?
Never. All page rendering and CropBox geometry updates execute 100% locally in your web browser memory. Your files never leave your computer or smartphone.
