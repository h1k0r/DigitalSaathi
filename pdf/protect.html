Select your file in the box above or drag it in. Type a secure password and re-enter to confirm. Select permissions to allow or prevent printing/copying, then click 'Encrypt & Protect PDF' to instantly save your file.

Questions and Answers

How do I password protect a PDF for free?
Simply drop your PDF into the file box on this page, enter your password twice, and click 'Encrypt & Protect PDF'. Your protected file is created directly on your device.
What happens if I forget my password?
Because our encryption uses standard cryptographic routines with zero knowledge on our end, we cannot recover forgotten passwords. Please ensure you remember or safely store the password you set.
Are my confidential files uploaded to any servers?
Never. Vytra runs entirely in your web browser. Neither your original file nor your password is ever sent over the network to any third-party server.
Can I later remove the password?
Yes! You can use our Unlock PDF tool. Just enter the password once, and Vytra will generate a clean, unprotected version of your PDF document.
