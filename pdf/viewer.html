Frequently Asked Questions

Is my PDF uploaded to an external server when using this viewer? +
No. Vytra PDF Viewer executes 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded, stored, or transmitted over the internet, guaranteeing complete privacy for confidential medical records, legal contracts, and financial statements.
Can I open large PDF files with hundreds of pages? +
Yes. Our high-performance PDF engine lazily renders pages on demand. Even multi-hundred-page textbooks, court briefs, or annual corporate reports load smoothly without freezing your computer or consuming excessive memory.
Can I switch between single page view and continuous scrolling? +
Yes. Use the view mode toggle on the top toolbar to switch between Single Page mode (ideal for presentations, certificates, and slide decks) and Continuous Scroll mode (ideal for books, research articles, and legal documents).
Can I print my document directly from this online reader? +
Yes. Simply click the 'Print' button on the toolbar or press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac) to invoke your browser's native print dialog and output high-resolution vector pages to any connected printer.
Does this PDF viewer work on iPhone, iPad, and Android phones? +
Yes. Vytra PDF Viewer is fully responsive and touch-friendly. You can open and read PDFs seamlessly on Safari (iOS), Google Chrome (Android), Samsung Internet, and mobile Firefox.
