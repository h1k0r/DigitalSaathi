Frequently Asked Questions

Does extracting pages degrade text quality or vector graphics in the PDF?
No. Vytra uses native PDF object copying via pdf-lib. All vector fonts, selectable text layers, embedded images, and exact page geometries are copied byte-for-byte without rasterization or quality loss.
How do I type custom page ranges like non-consecutive pages?
You can type page numbers separated by commas and dashes in the range input field. For example, '1-4, 7, 10-12' extracts pages 1 through 4, page 7, and pages 10 through 12 in order.
What is the difference between Split PDF and Extract PDF?
Split PDF typically divides a document into multiple individual files (such as splitting every 5 pages). Extract PDF lets you cherry-pick specific individual pages to combine into a single, clean output document.
Are my sensitive PDF contracts or financial statements uploaded to any server?
Never. All page rendering and PDF assembly happen 100% client-side inside your browser's WebAssembly and JavaScript environment. Your documents never touch an external server.
