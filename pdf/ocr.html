Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vytra extract text from PDF files? +
Vytra uses PDF.js and WebAssembly text stream parsers to decode character mappings, FontFile glyph tables, and text layout matrix coordinates directly in your browser. This yields a 99.9% exact character match without sending your file to any cloud server.
Can I search for specific words or terms inside the extracted text? +
Yes. Our OCR workspace includes a real-time keyword search box. Typing any phrase or number immediately highlights all occurrences in bright yellow throughout the multi-page transcript.
Are my sensitive PDF contracts or financial statements uploaded anywhere? +
No. 100% of the text extraction runs locally in your web browser memory. Your confidential documents never leave your computer, ensuring absolute privacy for sensitive audits, court pleadings, and personal records.
Can I copy the extracted text or download it as a text file? +
Yes. You can click '📋 Copy All Text' to copy everything to your operating system clipboard with a single click, or click '⬇️ Download .TXT' to save an offline plain-text copy formatted by page numbers.
Is this OCR tool completely free without page limits? +
Yes. Vytra provides unlimited free access to our client-side OCR tool with zero document limits, no registration, and no watermarks.
