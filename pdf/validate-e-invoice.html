Frequently Asked Questions

What checks does the e-invoice validator perform?

The validator checks three essential tiers: (1) XML/JSON syntax and well-formedness, (2) Schema & profile compliance (e.g. UN/CEFACT CII 100, OASIS UBL 2.1, NIC v1.03), and (3) Core business terms (invoice number, issue date, valid VAT/GSTIN IDs, line items, and mathematical equality of sums).

What is the European Standard EN 16931?

EN 16931 is the mandatory European standard establishing the semantic data model for electronic invoices across the European Union. Both ZUGFeRD 2.2 / Factur-X and Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 UBL conform directly to this norm.

Can I validate Indian GST e-Invoices with this tool?

Yes. You can paste or upload your official GST e-Invoice JSON payload. The validator checks the presence of SellerDtls, BuyerDtls, 15-digit GSTIN formats, ItemList calculations, and ValDtls consistency according to NIC Schema v1.03.

Are my confidential commercial invoices uploaded to your servers?

No. The entire validation algorithm executes inside your browser using JavaScript and the Web Crypto API for document hashing. No file or sensitive financial data is ever transmitted over the network.

What is the Validation Certificate?

The Validation Certificate is a downloadable vector PDF report detailing the results of the automated audit, including the SHA-256 cryptographic checksum of your file, timestamp, list of passed tests, and conformity status for audit compliance.

Is this tool free for corporate and commercial use?

Yes, completely free. There are no monthly validation limits, signups, or subscription fees.
