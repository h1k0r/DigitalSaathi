Frequently Asked Questions

How do I generate an invoice PDF on Vytra?

Enter your business name, address, and GSTIN/Tax ID in the seller section, add client billing details, input your line items with quantities and unit rates, select the tax structure, and click "Generate & Download Invoice PDF". A high-resolution A4 PDF will be generated immediately in your browser.

Is my billing and client data confidential?

Yes, 100%. All processing and calculations run locally inside your browser memory using JavaScript. None of your business details, customer information, rates, or totals are ever transmitted to or stored on our servers.

How does Indian GST (CGST, SGST, IGST) calculation work?

When you select "Intra-State GST", taxes are split equally into 9% CGST and 9% SGST (or selected rate) on the taxable subtotal. If you select "Inter-State GST", an integrated 18% IGST is applied, matching Indian statutory compliance.

Can I add multiple line items, discounts, and payment terms?

Yes, you can click "+ Add Line Item" to add as many products or service entries as you need. You can also specify discounts, bank account numbers, IFSC codes, UPI handles, and payment conditions.

Does Vytra charge any subscription fee or place watermarks?

No. Vytra is 100% free with zero watermarks and no hidden subscriptions. You can generate and download as many invoices as your business requires.

Can I create invoices on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes! Vytra is fully mobile-responsive. You can create, edit, and download invoices directly on Android Chrome, iPhone Safari, iPads, and desktop computers.
