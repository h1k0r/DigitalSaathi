Frequently Asked Questions

How do I edit text on the visual invoice sheet?

Simply click on any text element on the A4 invoice sheet - such as company name, client name, invoice number, item description, or pricing - and type directly. The sheet uses HTML5 ContentEditable technology.

How do I add or remove line item rows?

Click the "+ Add Row to Invoice" button located just below the items table to insert a new row. To delete an item, click the small red cross icon next to that row.

Can I upload my own company logo?

Yes! Click on the "+ Add Logo" box in the top right corner of the invoice sheet. Select any PNG, JPG, or SVG image from your computer to embed your brand logo immediately.

Does this tool work without an active internet connection?

Yes! Once the page is loaded in your browser, all editing, recalculations, and PDF exports run 100% locally on your machine without needing server connectivity.

Can I print directly without saving as PDF first?

Yes, click the "Print Direct" button in the visual toolbar to launch your browser's native print dialog formatted specifically for clean A4 printing.

Is there any cost or limit on how many invoices I can create?

No. Vytra's visual invoice builder is 100% free with unlimited usage, zero subscriptions, and no forced watermarks.
