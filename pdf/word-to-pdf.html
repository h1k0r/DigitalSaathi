Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding Word to PDF conversion on Vytra.

How does the Word to PDF converter operate without uploading files to a cloud server? +
Vytra executes OpenXML document parsing entirely inside your web browser using Mammoth.js and jsPDF. Your .docx file is decoded in local browser memory and rendered directly to vector and raster PDF buffers without ever transmitting data across the internet.
Are my confidential contracts, legal dockets, or resumes secure? +
Yes, 100%. Because all processing occurs locally on your computer, your documents never touch external servers, databases, or cloud storage. It complies with GDPR, HIPAA, and strict corporate NDA confidentiality requirements.
Does the converter retain tables, lists, and embedded images? +
Yes. The Mammoth.js OpenXML engine faithfully extracts tabular structures, bullet points, numbered lists, text formatting (bold, italic, underline), and embedded JPEG and PNG images.
How does multi-page pagination work? +
Our pagination algorithm computes the exact vertical dimensions of your document content and automatically slices continuous Word text across standard ISO 216 A4 pages with consistent margins and clean page breaks.
Do I need Microsoft Word or an Office 365 subscription installed? +
No. The converter runs autonomously in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iPhone without requiring Microsoft Word or third-party desktop software.
Can I convert older .doc files as well as newer .docx files? +
The primary engine is optimized for modern standard .docx OpenXML files. For older legacy binary .doc files (Word 97-2003), we recommend saving as .docx first or converting via our complementary text workflow.
