Frequently Asked Questions

What is PDF Form Flattening, and why should I do it? +
PDF form flattening converts interactive AcroForm text fields, checkboxes, and radio buttons into permanent, static page elements. When a form is flattened, the filled-in answers become part of the printable document stream and cannot be modified, deleted, or altered by anyone opening the file later.
Why does my PDF show 'No interactive fields detected'? +
If a PDF was generated by scanning physical paper or saved without digital form fields, it does not have interactive AcroForm widgets. For these scanned forms, you can easily use the Vytra Edit PDF or Sign PDF tool to type text anywhere directly on top of the document.
Are my sensitive personal form details uploaded to any cloud server? +
No. Vytra PDF Forms runs 100% locally inside your web browser using WebAssembly. Your personal identity numbers, addresses, banking details, and form responses are processed entirely in your computer's RAM and never transmitted across the network.
Does this tool support dropdown lists and multiple checkboxes? +
Yes. Our engine automatically parses standard PDF AcroForm structures including single-line text fields, multi-line address blocks, checkboxes, dropdown select lists, and radio button options.
Can I leave the form interactive without flattening it? +
Yes. Simply uncheck the 'Flatten Form' option before saving. Your filled data will be saved into the document while keeping all fields interactive for future edits in Adobe Acrobat or browser viewers.
