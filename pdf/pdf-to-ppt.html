Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding PDF to PowerPoint conversion on Vytra.

How do I import the extracted slides into Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides? +
Download the ZIP archive and extract the numbered slide graphics. In Microsoft PowerPoint, navigate to Insert > Photo Album > New Photo Album, select the extracted slide images, and PowerPoint will instantly assemble a complete presentation deck with one slide per page.
What resolution are the extracted presentation slides? +
Slides are rendered at Full HD 1080p (1920×1080 equivalent) by default, with an optional Ultra HD 4K setting. This guarantees razor-sharp presentation graphics even when projected on large auditorium screens.
Are my company's confidential pitch decks safe from third-party AI scrapers? +
Yes, 100%. The rendering and slide extraction runs entirely inside your web browser using client-side WebAssembly. No slide imagery or text is ever uploaded to any cloud server or database.
Can I download individual slides rather than the entire presentation? +
Yes. Each slide card in the gallery includes a direct 1-click download link, allowing you to save specific keynote diagrams or graphs individually without extracting the entire deck.
Can I edit text directly in the converted slides? +
Because PDFs frequently flatten layout layers and embedded fonts, this tool converts each page into a high-DPI slide backdrop. If you need fully editable text and paragraph extraction, please use our companion PDF to Word Tool.
Is there a slide limit or file size cap? +
No. You can convert 10-slide startup decks or 100-slide university lecture series directly on your computer without software subscriptions or paywalls.
