Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vytra reorder PDF pages without quality loss? +
DigitalSaathi operates at the internal PDF object level using PDF-Lib. When you sort or rotate pages, our engine copies the underlying vector streams and structural fonts directly without rasterizing pages into blurry image bitmaps. The resulting document retains 100% original sharpness and selectable text.
Can I delete specific blank pages or separator sheets from a scan? +
Yes. Each page card features a dedicated trash can icon (️). Simply click delete on any unwanted blank sheets or cover pages to permanently exclude them from the saved PDF.
Can I rotate individual pages that were scanned upside down? +
Yes. Every page thumbnail card contains independent 90° clockwise and counter-clockwise rotation buttons. You can rotate individual pages or use the 'Rotate All' buttons in the toolbar to adjust the entire document.
Do my private documents upload to any cloud servers? +
No. All PDF page thumbnail generation and file assembly execute 100% locally inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. Zero bytes are uploaded to remote servers, ensuring total privacy.
Is there a limit on how many pages I can organize? +
Vytra PDF Organizer supports documents with dozens to hundreds of pages smoothly. The tool is 100% free with unlimited usage and zero watermarks.
