How to rotate PDF pages

Upload your PDF file above. Click the ↺ or ↻ buttons on individual page cards, or use the top bulk buttons to rotate all pages. Click 'Save & Download' to download your updated document.

Questions and Answers

Will the rotation stay when I send the PDF to someone else?
Yes. Vytra writes the exact rotation degree into the PDF document definition. When you email the file or upload it to a portal, it will open in the saved orientation on any device.
Can I rotate a PDF 180 degrees?
Yes! Click the 'Rotate All 180°' button in the toolbar, or click the individual 90° button twice on any specific page thumbnail to flip it upside down.
Are there limits on file size or number of pages?
No artificial limits. Because all processing runs inside your browser, documents with hundreds of pages can be rotated seamlessly without timeout errors.
Is this tool free?
Yes, Vytra Rotate PDF is 100% free with no subscription, no hidden charges, and no email required.
