Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert a PDF to JPG or PNG without installing software?
Simply upload your PDF file to Vytra's online converter. Choose between JPG, PNG, or WebP formats, and click "Convert to Images". You can download each page individually or all pages together in a ZIP file immediately.
Are my confidential PDF documents uploaded to a remote server?
No. Unlike other tools that send your files to external cloud servers, Vytra runs 100% locally in your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly. Your documents never leave your device.
Can I convert multiple pages and download them in a single ZIP file?
Yes! Whether your PDF has 2 pages or 200 pages, Vytra converts every page and provides a "Download All as ZIP" button to save all extracted images in a single archive.
What image resolution (DPI) does Vytra export?
You can choose between Standard Quality (1x, ~72 DPI) for smaller files, Medium Quality (1.5x, ~150 DPI) for general sharing, and High Definition (2x, ~300 DPI) for crystal-clear printing and presentation needs.
