Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between standard PDF and PDF/A? +
Standard PDF is optimized for dynamic screen display and may rely on external system fonts, interactive JavaScript, audio/video streams, and uncalibrated RGB colors. PDF/A (ISO 19005) is an internationally standardized subset designed specifically for digital preservation. It mandates embedded fonts, device-independent color spaces (via OutputIntents), universal XMP metadata, and strips non-deterministic interactive scripting so the document renders identically 50 years from now.
Which PDF/A profile is accepted by Indian High Courts and e-Courts? +
PDF/A-1b (ISO 19005-1 Level B) is the mandated standard for e-filing in the Supreme Court of India, High Courts (Delhi, Bombay, Allahabad, Madras), and District e-Courts portals. It guarantees visual conformance across any operating system.
Are my legal contracts or thesis documents uploaded to any server? +
No. Vytra PDF/A Converter runs 100% locally inside your web browser using WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud, ensuring 100% compliance with client confidentiality and privacy regulations.
Does PDF/A conversion alter the visible content or formatting of my pages? +
No. PDF/A standardization preserves exact visual geometry, typography, and page layout. It injects the ISO OutputIntent color profile, applies standardized XMP archival headers, and locks deterministic rendering properties without altering your original text or drawings.
