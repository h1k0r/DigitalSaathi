Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vytra repair corrupted or unreadable PDF files?
Many PDF errors are caused by broken cross-reference (XRef) tables, truncated EOF markers, or corrupted trailers. Vytra uses a permissive parsing engine to locate surviving object streams, rebuilds the internal XRef index, and re-encodes a clean, standard-compliant PDF document.
Can all broken PDF files be 100% restored?
Vytra can successfully repair structural corruption, incomplete file transfers, and damaged indexing. However, if the underlying raw byte data has been completely overwritten with zeros or permanently lost, lost page content cannot be recreated.
Why does Adobe Acrobat say 'The file is damaged and could not be repaired'?
Adobe Acrobat strictly enforces exact byte offsets specified in the trailer dictionary. If a single byte is shifted during network transfer, Acrobat rejects the file. Vytra bypasses rigid index checks and parses raw objects directly to extract surviving pages.
Are my confidential files uploaded or saved on any server?
Never. All diagnostic scanning and PDF rebuilding happen 100% locally in your device RAM using WebAssembly. Your files never touch an external server.
