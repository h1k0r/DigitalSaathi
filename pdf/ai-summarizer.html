Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about client-side AI PDF summarization on Vytra.

How does the AI PDF Summarizer work without sending files to OpenAI or cloud servers? +
Vytra executes a high-speed Natural Language Processing (NLP) heuristic engine right inside your web browser. When you drop a PDF, the document text layer is extracted into local browser memory using PDF.js. An extractive summarization algorithm calculates term frequency, conceptual weights, and sentence significance scores entirely on your device's CPU. No document content is ever transmitted over the network.
Is there a page count or file size limit for PDF summarization? +
There are no artificial limits. Unlike server-hosted AI tools that enforce strict 4,000-token caps or charge per thousand words, Vytra runs client-side. You can summarize lengthy 300-page textbooks, corporate policy manuals, or financial disclosures without encountering page caps or subscription paywalls.
Are my confidential contracts, financial audits, or personal data secure? +
Yes, completely. Because our engine runs 100% locally in your browser's client sandbox, your files never touch any external server, cloud database, or AI training repository. It complies naturally with enterprise non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and privacy standards like GDPR and India's DPDP Act.
Can I summarize scanned paper documents or photo-based PDFs? +
This tool requires an underlying text layer in the PDF. If your PDF is an image scan from a scanner or smartphone camera, please run it through our free OCR PDF Tool first to decode the character layer, and then summarize the output file here.
How does the algorithm pick which sentences are the most important? +
The algorithm strips grammatical stop words ('the', 'and', 'with') and computes the frequency distribution of domain-specific nouns and verbs across the text. Each sentence receives a score based on keyword density, sentence length, and position weighting (sentences appearing in executive summaries, introductions, and conclusions receive a calibrated scoring multiplier).
What export formats are available for the summarized digest? +
You can copy the entire summary or individual bullet points to your clipboard with one click. Additionally, you can export the summary as an organized Markdown file (.md), a lightweight Plain Text file (.txt), or generate a clean printable PDF Executive Brief via our integrated jsPDF engine.
