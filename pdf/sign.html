How to sign PDF files

Select your PDF document or drag it into the file box. Draw your signature on the pad or type with cursive handwriting fonts. Click anywhere on the document to position your signature, then click 'Sign & Save PDF'.

✍️

Draw, type, or upload

Choose between hand-drawing your signature on mouse/touchscreen, typing your name with elegant cursive fonts, or uploading a scanned signature image with transparent background.

Easy to use

Legally recognized

Creates standard digital PDF overlays that comply with electronic signature guidelines for employment contracts, rental agreements, and forms.

All signature rendering and PDF embedding happens purely inside your local browser sandbox. Your signature and document are never sent to external servers.
