Frequently Asked Questions

Can I count pages across multiple PDF files simultaneously in batch?
Yes! You can select or drop dozens of PDF documents at once. Vytra will read the page count of each document in parallel and display an aggregate total alongside a detailed per-file breakdown table.
How does the printing cost calculator work?
The calculator multiplies your total page count by your custom rate per page (default ₹2 for standard B&W print) and your copies multiplier. You can adjust the rate per page to match your local cyber café or commercial printing shop tariff.
Does the counter inspect PDF title, author, and producer metadata?
Yes. Vytra reads the internal PDF Info dictionary and displays the document Title, Creator, and Producer software directly in the summary table.
Are my PDF documents uploaded to an external server for counting?
Never. All page counting and metadata reading execute 100% locally in your browser memory using PDF-Lib. Your private documents are never sent across the internet.
