Frequently Asked Questions

How is Vytra different from iLovePDF, Smallpdf, or Adobe Acrobat?

Unlike traditional PDF tools that require uploading your private files to remote servers, DigitalSaathi runs 100% inside your web browser via WebAssembly and PDF-lib. This means instant zero-wait execution, zero risk of data leaks, unlimited usage with no daily quotas, and no subscription fees.

Are my confidential PDF documents safe and private?

Absolutely. Since all operations take place strictly within your device's memory, neither your documents nor the processed files are ever sent to our servers or stored anywhere in the cloud.

Can I compress PDF documents to under 100KB for government job portals?

Yes! Our PDF Compressor features dedicated presets for Indian exam portals including SSC CGL, UPSC, IBPS, State PSC, and university portals to ensure your files pass portal upload checks without rejection.

Can I use these PDF tools on mobile phones and tablets?

Yes, all Vytra tools are fully touch-optimized and responsive. You can merge, split, compress, or sign PDF files seamlessly on Android Chrome, iPhone Safari, iPads, and desktop computers.

Are there any file size limits or paid subscriptions?

No. All 35+ PDF tools are completely free to use forever with no hidden paywalls, no subscriptions, and no registration required.

What is the PDF Workflow tool?

The PDF Workflow tool lets you chain multiple PDF operations (such as stamping page numbers, applying a watermark, and flattening forms) into a single automated pipeline with 1-click execution.
