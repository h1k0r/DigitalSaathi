Questions and Answers

Can I customize the opacity of the watermark?
Yes. Use the Opacity slider to set the watermark transparency anywhere from subtle 10% (almost transparent background stamp) to 90% (opaque foreground stamp).
What image formats are supported for logo stamps?
Both PNG and JPG images are supported. PNG with transparent alpha background works best so the underlying document text remains legible.
Does watermarking apply to all pages in the PDF?
Yes, the configured watermark is systematically applied across every page of your PDF document.
Can anyone easily remove the watermark?
The watermark is baked into the PDF content stream. Standard readers and viewers cannot select or remove the watermark without specialized vector editing tools.
