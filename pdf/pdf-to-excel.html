Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding PDF to Excel conversion on Vytra.

How does the converter extract tables from PDFs that have no visible borders? +
Our algorithm uses 2D spatial coordinate quantization. Rather than relying on drawn borders, it groups text elements by vertical Y positions into discrete rows, then orders characters along the horizontal X axis into aligned column buckets.
Can I convert multi-page bank statements into a single Excel worksheet? +
Yes. The converter parses every page in the uploaded PDF and stitches all table rows sequentially into one continuous worksheet ready for financial analysis.
Are my financial figures, transactions, and statements safe? +
Yes, 100%. The entire coordinate extraction and XLSX compilation executes locally within your web browser's memory sandbox. Your bank statements and invoices never leave your computer.
Can I convert scanned paper receipts or image-only PDFs? +
This tool requires a digital text layer in the PDF. If your PDF is a flat photo or camera scan, first run it through our free OCR PDF Tool to decode characters, then extract the table here.
Can I use formulas like SUM() immediately after downloading the .xlsx? +
Yes. Our SheetJS export engine automatically sanitizes currency symbols and trailing spaces so numbers are recognized as numerical cells by Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
Is there a row or file size limit for PDF to Excel conversion? +
No. Because extraction is computed locally on your device, you can process statements with thousands of rows without encountering page limits or paywalls.
