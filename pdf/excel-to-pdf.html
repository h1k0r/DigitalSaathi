Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding Excel to PDF conversion on Vytra.

How do wide Excel spreadsheets fit onto standard A4 or Letter PDF pages? +
Our converter uses jsPDF AutoTable column scaling. It computes the maximum character length in each column and scales cell widths proportionally to prevent horizontal overflow and awkward column clipping.
Can I convert specific individual sheets from a multi-sheet workbook? +
Yes. When you load an Excel workbook containing multiple tabs, our tool lists all sheet names in an interactive dropdown, allowing you to preview and export exactly the tab you need.
Are my corporate financial figures and payroll records uploaded to any server? +
No. The entire workbook parsing and PDF compilation runs locally inside your web browser using SheetJS and jsPDF. No spreadsheet data ever leaves your computer's RAM.
Does the converter support CSV and TSV files in addition to XLSX? +
Yes. You can upload Comma-Separated Values (.csv), Tab-Separated Values (.tsv), modern Excel OpenXML (.xlsx), and legacy Excel (.xls) files.
Is there a limit on the number of rows or columns? +
There are no arbitrary software limits. You can convert spreadsheets with hundreds of rows; our AutoTable engine automatically adds sequential pages with repeating table header rows.
Do I need Microsoft Excel or Office 365 installed? +
No. The tool runs autonomously in your web browser on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS without requiring Microsoft Office or any desktop software.
