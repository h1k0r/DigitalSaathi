Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding PDF to Word conversion on Vytra.

Can I edit the converted Word file directly in Microsoft Word and Google Docs? +
Yes. The generated file is packaged with Microsoft Word document XML namespaces (xmlns:w, xmlns:o). When you open it in Microsoft Word, LibreOffice Writer, or Google Docs, you can freely edit text, rewrite paragraphs, modify fonts, and insert new sections.
How does the converter prevent fragmented, broken lines in paragraphs? +
Unlike primitive text copiers that break text after every 60 characters, our heuristic parser analyzes vertical coordinate deltas and trailing punctuation. It stitches contiguous lines into continuous, flowing Word paragraphs.
Are my confidential contracts, taxes, or personal documents secure? +
Yes, 100%. The entire conversion pipeline runs strictly in your web browser's local sandbox memory. Neither your PDF nor your converted Word document is ever uploaded to a server or shared with third parties.
Can I convert scanned PDF documents into Word? +
This converter operates on documents that possess an underlying digital text layer. If your PDF is a flat image scan, first run it through our free OCR PDF Tool to decode the text, then convert it to Word here.
Is there a page count or file size limit for PDF to Word conversion? +
No. Because our converter runs on your local computer's processing power, there are no artificial token restrictions, document size limits, or per-page charges.
What formatting is preserved in the converted Word document? +
The converter preserves heading levels (Title, H1, H2), paragraph spacing, bulleted list markers, line breaks, and page separator markers.
