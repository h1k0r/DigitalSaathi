Questions and Answers

Can I skip numbering on the cover or title page?
Yes! Under 'Cover Page Handling', select 'Skip Page 1 (Cover page without number)'. The first page will remain completely clean without any stamped numbers, and the second physical page will begin numbering.
Does this tool support 'Page X of Y' total page formatting?
Yes. Vytra automatically calculates the exact page count of your uploaded PDF and provides dynamic formats such as 'Page {n} of {total}' or '{n} / {total}'.
Can I choose where the numbers appear on the pages?
Yes, our position matrix lets you place the numbers in any of 6 slots: Top Left, Top Center, Top Right, Bottom Left, Bottom Center, or Bottom Right with safe margin clearance.
Are my PDF files uploaded to any third-party servers?
Never. Vytra runs 100% locally in your client web browser memory using PDF-Lib WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded, stored, or indexed on remote servers.
