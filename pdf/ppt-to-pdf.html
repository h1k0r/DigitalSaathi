Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding slide to PDF conversion on Vytra.

How do I convert PowerPoint (.pptx) or Keynote slides into a PDF handout? +
You can upload slide export files (JPEG, PNG, or SVG) or drag your slide deck directly into Vytra. Our client-side jsPDF compiler positions and scales slides into standard ISO A4 landscape or portrait pages.
Can I print multiple slides per page with lines for taking notes? +
Yes. You can select the 2-Slides Per Page or 4-Slides Per Page handout layout. The compiler formats slides with structured margins, leaving space for audience note-taking.
Will 16:9 widescreen slides fit neatly on standard A4 or Letter paper? +
Yes. Our geometry calculation automatically preserves the exact 16:9 widescreen or 4:3 standard aspect ratio of each slide, centering content with balanced white margins so no visual elements are distorted or cropped.
Are my confidential company roadmaps and investor pitch decks secure? +
Yes, 100%. The entire presentation compilation executes locally in your web browser. Neither your slides nor your final PDF is uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud service.
Can I reorder or remove individual slides before creating the PDF? +
Yes. Our interactive workspace shows thumbnail cards for every slide. You can remove unwanted slides with one click or add additional slides to the deck before generating your PDF.
Is there a slide limit when compiling PDF presentations? +
There are no arbitrary slide caps. You can compile short 10-slide pitches or 150-slide university lecture series directly on your computer.
