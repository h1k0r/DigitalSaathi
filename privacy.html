Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use DigitalSaathi.

1. Local Processing (No Uploads)

Our core philosophy is privacy first. Almost all of our tools (PDF compression, image resizing, resume building, etc.) operate entirely on your device. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your sensitive files, photos, and personal documents are never uploaded to our servers.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect personal data unnecessarily. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies for our tools. We may use standard, privacy-friendly web analytics to understand overall traffic patterns and improve the platform.

3. Third-Party Links

Our platform contains links to official government websites, job portals, or third-party resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites. We encourage you to read their respective privacy policies.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
