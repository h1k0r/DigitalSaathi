Why the IBPS signature fails more than the photo

IBPS forms validate the signature in a narrow window: 10 to 20 KB, a small wide image (roughly 140×60 pixels, the 7:3 shape of a 4×2 cm signature area), as JPG. Narrow windows fail more often — there is simply less room to land in. A 40 KB photo overshoots by 80%; a 25 KB signature overshoots by only 25%, but it fails exactly the same way. The fix is not effort, it is aiming: type 15 into the target above and let the encoder search.

Signature size 20KB se zyada hai? Ye karo

Three moves, in order. One: crop tight around the signature — most oversized signature files carry more white paper than ink, and paper is exactly what compression hates least. Two: keep the white-background cleanup switched on (it is on by default here): grey paper under window light becomes pure white, deep ink becomes crisper black, the middle tones of your strokes stay untouched. Three: let the preset's 15 KB target do the quality search. The result downloads as Signature_15KB.jpg — verify the number in your file manager and upload with confidence.

Shoot once, reuse for every IBPS cycle

The source photo matters more than any setting. Sign in dark ink on plain white paper; no lines, no grid, no date beside it. Photograph it straight-on in even indirect light — flash creates a hot spot on ballpoint ink and a shadow ridge at the paper edge. One clean capture resized per-form serves PO, Clerk and RRB cycles for years. If your style has drifted since an older signature, re-sign fresh rather than reusing the old scan: verification compares your form signature against your record, and consistency beats freshness.

Format and field gotchas

  • JPG only in most cycles: PNG screenshots of a signature scan get rejected by fields expecting JPG. This tool outputs JPG always.
  • Separate from the photo: IBPS asks photo and signature as two different files with two different bands — never paste both into one image.
  • The declaration is not the signature: the handwritten declaration is its own upload, usually a longer text on its own page — the photo-to-PDF workflow in JPG to PDF covers that file.
  • Check before the deadline, not at it: run the final file through the Pre-flight Checker — size, format, ratio and background in one pass.

Numbers above are compiled from recent notifications (source linked in the table, last verified shown there). Different IBPS cycles word their bands slightly differently — when the live form's instruction disagrees, the form wins.

The night-before routine

Do not resize on submission day. The evening before the form opens (or the evening before you plan to fill it), prepare the full file set: photo at 20–50 KB, signature at 15 KB, both named with their sizes, both run through the Pre-flight Checker. This single habit converts the most stressful twenty minutes of the process — the deadline-hour scramble where people upload the wrong file into the wrong field — into a five-minute drag-and-drop of already- verified files. If something unexpected appears (a declaration upload, a left-thumb impression field), you still have calm hours left to handle it instead of minutes.

One more pattern worth knowing: fields reject more often on the second attempt after a failed upload, because candidates re-upload the same wrong file or over-compress an already- compressed one. Always go back to the original and resize fresh. Compressing a compressed file compounds quality loss for zero size benefit — the encoder has nothing left to give. The tool above always starts from your original drop; keep it in front of you.

3 common rejections — and their fix

“File size should be between 10 KB and 20 KB”

Wajah: The signature is over 20 KB (untrimmed scan) or under 10 KB (over-compressed, ink eaten away).

Fix: Target the middle: the preset above uses 15 KB. Re-download and confirm the size in your file manager before retrying.

“Signature image dimensions are not as prescribed”

Wajah: Wrong pixel shape — too tall, too square, or built from a full A4 scan instead of a tight crop.

Fix: Crop tightly around the signature, then resize: the IBPS preset produces the wide ~140×60 shape the fields expect.

“Signature is not clear / cannot be recognised”

Wajah: Grey or shadowed paper, motion blur, or an ink tone that reads as light grey after earlier compression.

Fix: Re-shoot flat in even light with dark ink; enable white-background cleanup here. Compression fixes background, not blur — if the strokes themselves are soft, recapture.

Common questions

IBPS signature ka exact size kya hai?
10–20 KB JPG, about 140×60 pixels (the shape of a 4×2 cm signature area). The preset above targets 15 KB — the safe middle of the band.
Signature banane ka sahi tarika kya hai?
Plain white paper, dark pen, straight-on photo in even light. No lines or date beside the signature. Crop tight, then resize here with the white-background cleanup on.
Kya main PNG upload kar sakta hoon?
Most IBPS cycles ask for JPG specifically. This tool outputs JPG always — drop any photo in and the download will be a correctly-sized JPG.
Photo aur signature ek hi file me daal sakte hain?
No — IBPS asks them as separate files with separate bands (photo typically 20–50 KB, signature 10–20 KB). Keep two files, named with their sizes.
White background cleanup signature ko bigad to nahi degi?
It only remaps near-white paper and deep ink tones; your strokes' mid-tones are preserved. It changes the paper, not what the signature says — and altering the signature itself is never acceptable.
Upload se pehle kaise verify karun?
Run the file through the Pre-flight Checker: it shows size band, format, ratio and background whiteness as pass/fail with plain reasons — the same mechanical checks the portal performs.
