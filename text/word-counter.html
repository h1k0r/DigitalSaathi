Frequently Asked Questions

How is reading time and speaking time calculated?
Silent reading speed is benchmarked at 200 words per minute (WPM), representing the average reading pace of an adult. Vocal speaking time is calculated at 130 words per minute, which is the industry standard cadence for clear keynote presentations, video voiceovers, and podcast narrations.
What is the difference between characters with spaces and without spaces?
Characters with spaces counts every letter, digit, punctuation mark, space, and tab. Characters without spaces ignores all whitespace characters. Academic thesis submissions, translation agencies, and publishing houses typically quote fees and requirements based on characters without spaces.
Is my text stored, analyzed, or sent to external servers?
No. The entire analysis occurs 100% inside your web browser memory. No text, word frequencies, or document contents are ever transmitted across the network, stored in databases, or logged on remote servers.
What is the ideal keyword density for SEO content?
Most SEO experts recommend maintaining a primary keyword density between 1.0% and 2.5%. Anything above 3% can trigger algorithmic over-optimization penalties from search engines like Google.
