Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard reading speed used for the reading time estimate? +
Silent reading speed is benchmarked at 225 words per minute (WPM), which is the verified global average for adults reading non-technical prose. Speaking or presentation pace is calculated at 130 WPM.
Does the tool count spaces as characters? +
Our tool provides both metrics simultaneously: total characters including spaces, and characters excluding whitespace, allowing you to comply with any journal or form guidelines.
Can I analyze large manuscripts over 50,000 words? +
Yes! Our client-side regex engine is optimized to parse 100,000+ words in under 100 milliseconds without freezing your browser.
