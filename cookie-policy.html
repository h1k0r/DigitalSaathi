Cookie Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar tracking technologies are, how Vytra (https://digitalsaathi.vytra.in/) uses them, and how you can control your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small data files stored on your computer or mobile device by your web browser when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide analytical or advertising information to the operators of the site.

Similar technologies include HTML5 localStorage and sessionStorage, which allow web applications to store preference keys directly inside your local browser memory without transmitting that data across the network on every HTTP request.

3. Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

Vytra participates in the Google AdSense advertising program. Google and its ad technology partners may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons to gather non-personally identifiable information while serving ads on our pages.

These cookies allow Google to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements based on your visit to Vytra and other sites across the web. You can review how Google processes advertising data by visiting Google's Advertising Privacy FAQ.

4. How Can You Control and Manage Cookies?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your preferences through several accessible methods:

A. Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to modify cookie settings via their settings or preference panels. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies, delete existing cookies, or alert you when a cookie is being sent:

  • Manage cookies in Google Chrome
  • Manage cookies in Mozilla Firefox
  • Manage cookies in Apple Safari
  • Manage cookies in Microsoft Edge

B. Targeted Advertising Opt-Out Portals

You can opt out of personalized advertising from participating ad networks through standard industry opt-out mechanisms:

  • Google Ads Settings: https://myadcenter.google.com/
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (US): https://optout.aboutads.info/
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EU/EEA): https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/
  • Network Advertising Initiative: https://optout.networkadvertising.org/
