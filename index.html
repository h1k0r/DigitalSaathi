Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Vytra better than traditional online tools like iLovePDF or Smallpdf?

Unlike traditional services that upload your private documents to third-party cloud servers with hourly usage caps, Vytra runs 100% inside your web browser using WebAssembly. This ensures complete privacy, zero upload wait times, and truly unlimited free conversions.

Are Vytra tools 100% free with no limits?

Yes! All 76+ tools on Vytra are completely free to use with no hidden subscriptions, no daily quotas, and no required registration.

Are my uploaded files safe and private?

Yes! Files never leave your device. All operations are executed locally within your web browser memory and sandbox.

Yes! Our tools feature built-in presets calibrated specifically for Indian examination and admission portals including SSC, UPSC, IBPS, and state government portals.
