Questions and Answers

Why is the CGPA multiplier 9.5 used by CBSE?
CBSE analyzed past board examination data and calculated that candidates scoring between 91 and 100 had an average percentage of 95. Dividing 95 by 10 yields the official statistical multiplier of 9.5.
Can I calculate percentage for 6 subjects?
Yes! Sum your marks across all 6 subjects and enter that into 'Obtained Marks', and set 'Total Marks' to 600.
How do I convert percentage back into CGPA?
Click the '% to CGPA' tab, type your percentage score, and click convert. The tool divides your percentage by 9.5 to determine your estimated CGPA on a 10-point scale.
