Frequently Asked Questions

What formula is used for calculating Home & Car Loan EMI? +
EMI is calculated using the standard reducing balance formula: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the principal amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12 and 100), and n is the total duration in months.
How does the 75% attendance rule calculator work? +
Enter your total classes held and classes attended. The tool calculates your current percentage. If you are below 75%, it calculates the exact number of consecutive upcoming classes you must attend: Classes Needed = (0.75 × Total - Attended) / 0.25. If you are above 75%, it shows how many classes you can afford to miss while remaining eligible.
How do I convert CGPA to percentage for college placements? +
For CBSE and most central universities, simply multiply your CGPA by 9.5. For example, an 8.4 CGPA equals 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.8%. If your university (such as Mumbai University or VTU) uses a specific conversion table or multiplier, you can easily customize the factor in our CGPA calculator.
