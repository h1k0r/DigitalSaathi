Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate classes needed to reach 75%?
Let 'A' be classes attended, 'T' be total classes held, and 'x' be consecutive classes you must attend. To reach 75%: (A + x) / (T + x) = 0.75. Solving for x yields: x = (0.75 × T - A) / 0.25 = 3T - 4A.
How do you calculate safe bunks?
If your current percentage is above 75%, let 'y' be the number of classes you can miss: A / (T + y) = 0.75. Solving for y yields: y = (A - 0.75 × T) / 0.75 = Math.floor((4A - 3T) / 3).
Do practical laboratory sessions count the same as theory lectures?
In many universities (like VTU and Anna University), lab sessions carry 2 or 3 contact hours per session. Missing one 3-hour lab counts as missing 3 classes in your attendance log. Always verify your department's hour accounting policy.
Can I get an admit card with 72% attendance?
Most universities have a provision for condonation of attendance for shortages between 65% and 75% on genuine medical grounds (hospitalization, contagious illness) or official university duty (NSS, NCC, sports tournaments), usually accompanied by a nominal condonation fee.
