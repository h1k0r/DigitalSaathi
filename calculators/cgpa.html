Frequently Asked Questions

How do you calculate CGPA from SGPA?
To calculate CGPA, multiply each semester's SGPA by its credit hours to get the credit points earned in that semester. Sum all credit points and divide by the total number of credits across all semesters: CGPA = Σ(SGPA × Credits) / Σ(Credits).
Why is CGPA multiplied by 9.5 to calculate percentage?
CBSE analyzed historical board examination data and established that candidates scoring between 91 and 100 marks averaged 95%. Dividing 95 by 10 yields the official conversion factor of 9.5. AICTE and several state technical universities adopted this identical factor.
What if all my semesters have equal credits?
If every semester has identical credits (e.g. 20 credits per semester), CGPA is simply the arithmetic average of all your semester SGPAs: CGPA = (SGPA 1 + SGPA 2 + ... + SGPA N) / N.
What is the minimum CGPA required for Campus Placements and PSUs?
Most Tier-1 tech companies (Google, Microsoft, Amazon) typically set an eligibility threshold of 7.0 or 7.5 CGPA. IT services firms (TCS, Infosys, Cognizant) require 6.0 CGPA (60%), while public sector undertakings (PSUs like ONGC, IOCL, BHEL) require a minimum 6.5 CGPA or 65% with no active backlogs.
