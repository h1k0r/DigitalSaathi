How to Calculate Your Exact Age

  1. Select your Date of Birth using the date picker.
  2. Optionally adjust the Age as of Date (defaults to today's date).
  3. Click Calculate Exact Age to see your age in years, months, days, plus total lived hours and next birthday countdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is exact chronological age calculated?

The calculator accounts for leap years, variations in days per month (28, 29, 30, or 31), and calculates the precise difference between the birth date and reference date.

Is my date of birth saved or stored on any server?

No. All calculations are executed 100% client-side in your web browser. No personal data or dates are ever sent to any server.
