Frequently Asked Questions

How is exact chronological age calculated?
Age is computed by subtracting the birth year, month, and day from the reference date. If the reference day is smaller than the birth day, days are borrowed from the previous calendar month (which may have 28, 29, 30, or 31 days). If the reference month is smaller than the birth month, 12 months are borrowed from the year.
What cut-off date should I use for SSC and UPSC forms?
Always refer to the official exam notification. For UPSC CSE, the cut-off date is typically August 1 of the exam year. For SSC CGL/CHSL, it is usually January 1 or August 1 depending on whether the notification is released in the first or second half of the year.
Does this calculator account for leap years?
Yes, leap years (including February 29) are fully supported. The day difference automatically factors in 366-day leap years throughout your lifespan.
Is my date of birth stored or shared?
No. All computations occur completely on your device using client-side JavaScript. No dates, queries, or user data are ever uploaded to any web server.
