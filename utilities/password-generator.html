Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a password cryptographically secure?
A password is cryptographically secure when it has sufficient length (16+ characters), high character entropy, and is generated using a Cryptographically Secure Pseudo-Random Number Generator (CSPRNG) like Web Crypto rather than predictable math functions like Math.random().
What is a Diceware passphrase and why is it recommended?
A Diceware passphrase chains multiple random dictionary words together separated by hyphens (e.g. silver-galaxy-beacon-forest). Because each word contributes approximately 13 bits of entropy, a 5-word passphrase has over 65 bits of entropy, making it extremely difficult to crack while remaining effortlessly memorable for humans.
Are any passwords transmitted or logged on external servers?
Never. The tool operates 100% locally inside your web browser. All random generation, entropy calculations, and clipboard copying take place on your local CPU without sending any network requests.
Does this tool work completely offline?
Yes. Once loaded, you can disconnect your Wi-Fi or mobile data entirely, and the generator will continue producing cryptographically secure passwords without interruption.
