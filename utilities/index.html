Frequently Asked Questions

Can I print the generated QR code for business signboards? +
Yes! Our QR code generator outputs crisp, high-resolution 1000×1000px PNG files suitable for print shops, shop counters, brochures, and standees without pixelation.
How strong are the generated passwords against brute-force attacks? +
A 16-character alphanumeric password with symbols generated by our tool has over 90 bits of entropy. At 100 trillion guesses per second, a supercomputer would require millions of years to crack it.
How does the WiFi QR code work when scanned by a phone? +
It follows the standard WIFI:S:SSID;T:WPA;P:Password;; protocol recognized natively by iOS Camera and Android Google Lens. When scanned, the phone automatically prompts to connect without typing the password.
