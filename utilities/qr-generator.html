Frequently Asked Questions

Do these generated QR codes expire or have scan limits?
No! Vytra generates genuine static QR codes. The destination URL or data is directly encoded into the graphic pattern itself without routing through intermediate redirect servers. They will work indefinitely with infinite scans.
How do I generate a UPI payment QR code?
Click on the 'UPI Pay' tab above. Enter your registered UPI ID (e.g. mobile@upi or name@bank) and your Payee Name. You can optionally pre-set an amount. Customers scanning with Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm will be taken straight to the payment screen.
Can all smartphones scan these QR codes?
Yes! iOS (iPhone Camera app) and Android (Google Lens / Camera app) natively recognize standard URLs, WiFi credentials, UPI payment links, and vCards without requiring any third-party scanner apps.
Is it safe to generate WiFi and payment QR codes here?
Yes, 100% safe. The QR code generator runs entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. No passwords, UPI IDs, or private data are ever transmitted or saved to external servers.
