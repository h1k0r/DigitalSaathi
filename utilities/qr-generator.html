QR Generator FAQ

Do generated QR codes expire?

No! These are standard static QR codes containing your direct encoded data. They never expire and have zero scan limits.

Is WiFi connection automatic when scanning?

Yes! Modern iOS and Android camera apps automatically prompt users with "Join Network" when scanning a WiFi QR code.
