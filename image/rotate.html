Frequently Asked Questions

Why do photos taken on phones upload sideways on government exam portals?
Smartphones record orientation in an internal EXIF metadata flag rather than physically rotating the pixel grid. Older government portals and application forms do not read EXIF data, causing images to display sideways. Vytra rewrites the raw pixel matrix so the image is universally upright everywhere.
Does rotating an image cause blurriness or loss of quality?
No. Standard 90-degree and 180-degree rotations simply transpose rows and columns of pixels across the canvas, preserving 100% of the original sharpness and clarity.
What does "Flip Horizontal" do?
Flipping horizontally creates a mirror image along the vertical axis (left becomes right and right becomes left). This is commonly used to correct inverted smartphone selfies or inverted scans.
Are my private photos uploaded to external servers?
Never. All image processing takes place 100% client-side inside your browser memory. Your documents and photographs are never sent over the internet or logged on any external server.
