Questions and Answers

What are the exact dimensions for Indian passport photos?
Indian passport and government forms require a 3.5cm width by 4.5cm height ratio (approximately 413×531 pixels at 300 DPI). Selecting our '3.5:4.5 Passport' button automatically locks this ratio.
Can I crop an image into a custom shape or freeform box?
Yes! With 'Freeform' mode selected, you can drag the corner and edges to crop any rectangular area freely without fixed aspect ratio constraints.
Can I compress the file size after cropping?
Yes! You can use our companion Image Compressor tool to reduce the cropped photo to exact file size limits (like under 20KB or 50KB for government forms).
Is this image cropping tool completely free?
Yes, Vytra Crop Image is 100% free with no watermarks, no registration, and unlimited usage.
