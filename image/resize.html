How to resize images

Select your image or drop it into the file box. Set your desired target width and height in pixels or click an exam portal preset. Save your newly resized image immediately.

Ensure your photos meet strict recruitment specifications like SSC (140x160 px), signature scans (140x60 px), or visa passport photos without any aspect distortion.
