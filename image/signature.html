Questions and Answers

What are the exact dimensions for SSC exam signatures?
SSC requires a JPEG signature image with width 140 pixels and height 60 pixels, and file size strictly between 10 KB and 20 KB. Selecting the SSC preset automatically configures these parameters.
How do I take a good photo of my signature?
Sign with a dark black or blue ballpoint/gel pen on unruled plain white paper. Ensure good overhead natural light to avoid phone shadows across the paper, then crop closely around the signature.
What happens if my original image is very large (e.g. 5 MB)?
Vytra automatically scales down the resolution to your selected pixel dimensions (e.g. 140×60) and performs intelligent compression to ensure the output file strictly complies with your chosen KB limit.
Is this tool free to use?
Yes, Vytra Signature Resizer is 100% free with unlimited conversions, zero watermarks, and no sign-in required.
