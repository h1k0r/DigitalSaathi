Questions and Answers

How do I copy a picked color directly to my clipboard?
Simply click on any of the color format cards (HEX, RGB, HSL, or CMYK), or click on any swatch in the dominant palette. The code is instantly copied to your operating system clipboard with a confirmation badge.
Why is CMYK color different from RGB on my screen?
RGB is an additive light model produced by illuminated computer displays with a wider color spectrum (gamut). CMYK is a subtractive ink model produced by physical ink pigments absorbing light on paper. Highly saturated electric neons in RGB are translated into the closest achievable CMYK print mix.
Does this tool save or upload my images to external servers?
Never. DigitalSaathi runs 100% locally in your web browser memory using HTML5 2D Canvas. Your pictures, sketches, and proprietary branding assets never leave your device.
Can I pick colors on mobile phones and tablets?
Yes! When touching the canvas on a smartphone, the loupe is dynamically positioned above your fingertip so your finger never blocks the pixel you are inspecting.
