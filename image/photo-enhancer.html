Questions and Answers

How do I remove the grey or yellow background from a scanned marksheet?
Upload the scan and click '📄 Document Scan Optimizer'. It boosts contrast while increasing highlight brightness, washing out grey scanner shadows and turning the background pure white while keeping printed text pitch black.
Can I make faint handwriting legible on old college notes?
Yes! Increase the Contrast slider to +75% or select the 'High-Contrast B&W' preset. This dramatically separates faint pencil or blue ink strokes from the paper fibers, making handwritten notes crisp and readable.
Will this tool change my image resolution or DPI?
No. The canvas dynamically matches the exact natural width and height of your original photo. Every single original pixel is preserved without downscaling or blurring.
Are my confidential marksheets stored or seen by anyone?
Never. Vytra runs 100% locally in your client web browser memory via HTML5 2D Canvas. Your personal marks, certificates, and ID documents never leave your computer or phone.
