Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to transparent pixels when converting from PNG to JPG?
Since JPEG does not have an alpha transparency channel, all transparent areas must be filled with a solid color. Vytra defaults to solid pure white (which is required by exam and identity portals), but you can also choose black or a custom color.
Why do exam portals require JPG instead of PNG?
Indian recruitment websites (like SSC, UPSC, and IBPS) handle millions of applicant photos simultaneously. Standardizing on JPG ensures minimal server storage and avoids software rendering crashes caused by complex PNG transparency maps.
Will converting PNG to JPG lower my photo resolution?
No. The width and height pixel dimensions remain 100% identical. The converter simply optimizes color table encoding according to your selected quality setting without resizing the dimensions.
Is my private photo uploaded to any server during conversion?
Never. The conversion executes entirely within your browser memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your documents and photographs are never sent over the internet or logged on any external server.
