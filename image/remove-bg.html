Frequently Asked Questions

Which background color is officially required for UPSC, SSC, and Indian government exams?
UPSC (Union Public Service Commission), SSC (Staff Selection Commission), NTA NEET/JEE, and IBPS officially require a plain, solid white or off-white background with no dark shadows, patterns, or objects visible behind the candidate.
Can I replace the background of a photo taken against a regular bedroom wall?
Yes. As long as your background has a relatively uniform color (like an off-white wall, curtain, or door), Vytra's eye-dropper tool lets you click directly on the wall to replace it with pure #FFFFFF white or light blue.
What does the Edge Feathering slider do?
Edge Feathering smooths out the transitional boundary pixels between your hair, ears, and clothing and the new background. This prevents harsh, pixelated, or artificial halos around your silhouette.
Are my personal passport photographs uploaded or stored on any server?
Never. All chroma processing and pixel recoloring execute 100% locally in your web browser memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your private photos never leave your computer or smartphone.
