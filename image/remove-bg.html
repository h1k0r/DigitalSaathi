Frequently Asked Questions

Why does Vytra allow full HD downloads while other sites charge for HD?

Other online background removers run on expensive cloud GPU servers, so they intentionally downscale your image to 0.25 megapixels (500x500px) and force you to buy credits for HD. Vytra runs 100% locally inside your browser's hardware memory using modern HTML5 Canvas, incurring zero cloud GPU costs and allowing you to download 100% full-resolution HD cutouts for free.

How do I export a transparent PNG cutout?

Simply keep the "Transparent" option selected under New HD Background. When you click "Download Transparent HD (PNG)", your file will be saved as a lossless PNG with full alpha channel transparency.

How do I get a pure white background for Amazon, Flipkart, or passport forms?

Click the "White (Amazon)" pill. The tool will substitute all background pixels with clean #FFFFFF studio white while applying edge anti-aliasing to prevent harsh borders.

How do the touch-up eraser and restore brushes work?

If any background shadow remains near shoulders or clothing, select "Touch-up Eraser" and paint over the unwanted area. If any part of the subject was mistakenly erased, select "Restore Subject Brush" and paint it right back in.

Are my confidential product designs or personal photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The entire background removal engine executes 100% inside your web browser. Your photos never leave your device.

Can I copy the HD transparent cutout directly to my clipboard?

Yes! Click the " Copy PNG" button to copy the transparent graphic directly into your clipboard, allowing you to paste (Ctrl+V) immediately into Canva, Photoshop, Figma, or Word documents.
