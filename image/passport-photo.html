Questions and Answers

What are the requirements for an Indian passport photo?
Indian passport specifications require a 3.5cm × 4.5cm photo with a plain white or off-white background, frontal view with eyes open, neutral facial expression, and head centered covering 70-80% of the image.
How do I print the A4 sheet?
Save the generated A4 JPEG file to your phone or pendrive. In printer settings, choose A4 paper size, select 100% scale (Do Not Scale / Actual Size), and use glossy photo paper for studio-quality prints.
What if my photo file size needs to be under 50KB?
After downloading your single passport photo, open our Image Compressor and choose the 'Under 50KB (Exam Portals)' preset to hit your target file size instantly.
Is this passport photo generator free?
Yes, Vytra Passport Photo Maker is 100% free with no watermarks and no registration required.
