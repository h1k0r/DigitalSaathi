Questions and Answers

Can blacked-out or blurred text be reversed or recovered?
No. When you redact on Vytra, the underlying RGB pixel values on the HTML5 canvas are permanently overwritten and flattened into the raster image file. There are no invisible layers, metadata caches, or hidden paths that could ever be reconstructed.
Which is safer: Solid Blackout or Mosaic Pixelation?
Solid Blackout is the safest redaction method for printed text and ID numbers because it leaves zero optical variation (all pixels become pure black RGB #000000). Mosaic Pixelation is excellent for faces and background objects where you want to signal context while obscuring identity.
Are my confidential documents uploaded to any remote server?
Never. All image rendering and pixel manipulation run strictly in your web browser memory. Your ID scans, bank statements, and private pictures never touch any server.
Can I copy the redacted picture directly to my clipboard?
Yes! Click the '📋 Copy Redacted Image to Clipboard' button to paste your censored document directly into an email, Slack, or WhatsApp Web chat without saving a file to disk.
