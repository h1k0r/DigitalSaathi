Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Image HD Converter work?

When you load an image, our tool applies progressive multi-pass bicubic resampling to scale up the pixel grid to 1080p, 2K, or 4K dimensions. It then executes an Unsharp Mask (USM) edge detection pass and micro-contrast clarity filter to restore edge sharpness and eliminate compression blurriness.

Can I convert WhatsApp compressed photos to HD?

Yes! WhatsApp and social media platforms heavily downscale and compress photos. Uploading them to Vytra's HD Converter scales them back to Full HD (1920x1080) and enhances clarity so they look crisp when displayed on monitors or printed.

What is the difference between 1080p, 2K, and 4K presets?

Full HD (1080p) scales your image's longest edge to 1920 pixels, perfect for desktop monitors and standard photo prints. 2K QHD (1440p) scales to 2560 pixels for high-density mobile screens. 4K Ultra HD (2160p) scales to 3840 pixels, ideal for large canvas prints, posters, and 4K displays.

Are my personal images uploaded to any server?

No. The entire conversion and filtering algorithm operates 100% inside your browser's local sandbox memory using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your device.

Can I set the output resolution to 300 DPI for printing?

Yes. You can select the 300 DPI or 600 DPI print setting before downloading, which formats the image for photo studio printers and government exam applications.

Is this Image HD Converter free to use?

Yes, completely free. There are no monthly subscriptions, hidden credits, or watermarks stamped onto your images.
