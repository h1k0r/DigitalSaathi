Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert PNG and WebP images to PDF as well?
Yes! Vytra natively supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP formats. You can even upload a mix of different formats simultaneously and compile them into a unified PDF.
Are my images uploaded or stored on any server?
No. The entire conversion runs 100% inside your local web browser. Your private IDs, salary slips, and confidential photos are never transferred across the network.
How do I reorder pages before generating the PDF?
Each uploaded image has intuitive "▲ Up" and "▼ Down" buttons in the list. Simply click them to rearrange the pages in whatever sequence you desire before clicking convert.
Is there any limit on how many images I can convert?
No. There are zero usage limits or artificial paywalls. You can convert as many photos as your device memory can accommodate without watermarks or charges.
