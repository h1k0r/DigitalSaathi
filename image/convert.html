Questions and Answers

When should I convert PNG to JPG?
Convert PNG to JPG when uploading photos to government, banking, or job portals (which often have strict 50KB or 100KB limits). JPG compresses photographic images much more efficiently than PNG.
Will transparent backgrounds turn black when converting PNG to JPG?
No. Vytra automatically renders a clean white background behind transparent areas when converting to JPG, so your images look pristine.
Why should I use WebP?
WebP is Google's modern image format supported by all major browsers. It produces file sizes that are 25-35% smaller than JPG while delivering equivalent visual fidelity.
Are there any limits on file count or size?
No limits. Because conversion happens locally in your browser memory, you can convert dozens of photos simultaneously without subscription fees.
