How to compress images

Select your JPG, PNG, or WebP photo or drop it into the upload box. Choose one of our exam portal presets (Under 20KB, 50KB, 100KB) or tweak the quality slider. Download your compressed photo in seconds.

Our intelligent binary search algorithm iteratively tests quality levels and dimensions to ensure your file strictly meets Indian government portal limits (SSC, UPSC, NTA, IBPS).
