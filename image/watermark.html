Questions and Answers

Can I watermark ID documents like Aadhaar, PAN card, or Voter ID for safety?
Yes! Government guidelines recommend stamping an explicit purpose like 'ONLY FOR KYC VERIFICATION - [RECIPIENT NAME] - [DATE]' across ID copies. Our diagonal tile pattern ensures the stamp covers the key credentials, making fraudulent misuse impossible while keeping the document legally valid.
Can I upload my own company logo as a watermark?
Yes! Click on the '🖼️ Logo / Image Overlay' tab. You can upload any transparent PNG, JPG, or SVG emblem and adjust its position and transparency slider to match your exact branding style.
Does this tool upload my personal photos to any server?
Never. All image processing runs 100% locally in your browser memory via the HTML5 2D Canvas API. Your pictures, confidential papers, and certificates never touch any external server.
What font size and opacity should I choose?
For anti-fraud document stamping (Aadhaar, certificates), an opacity of 30% to 40% with dark contrast stroke is ideal so underlying text remains readable. For photography logos, 50% to 70% in the bottom-right corner looks clean and professional.
